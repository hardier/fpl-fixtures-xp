How xP is calculated

FPL does not publish an expected-points figure, so this is our own model. For each fixture in the target gameweek it combines expected minutes (from starts and minutes per team game, scaled by the injury flag) with the player's per-90 rates for xG, xA, saves, defensive contributions, bonus and cards. Goal expectations come from the two clubs' attack and defence strength ratings and home advantage; clean-sheet and goals-conceded terms use a Poisson model on expected goals conceded. Double gameweeks are summed, blanks score zero. Players with under ~5 full games of minutes are blended towards FPL's own ep_this figure.
